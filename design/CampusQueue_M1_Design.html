CampusQueue – Milestone 1 UX and UI design

Wireframes and high-fidelity screens for Login, Register and Home. Frames are 390 × 844.

Style guide

Colours

Navy (primary)#1F3A5F · headers, status bar
Green (accent)#1E7B50 · main buttons, Open status
Background#F7F8FA · screens
Text#1B1F24 · body text
Muted#5F6B7A · hints, captions
Error#B3261E · validation
Warning#8A5A00 · Paused status

Text sizes

Title 24sp bold

Heading 20sp

Body 16sp

Caption 14sp

Spacing and sizes

8dp grid: 8, 16, 24dp

Touch targets: 48dp minimum

Buttons: 56dp tall, 4dp corners

Form width: 420dp maximum, centred

Accessibility

Contrast of white text on navy and green passes WCAG AA.

Status uses a word and a colour.
